stoprock simulator

The real firmware, running in Espressif's esp-emulator on an ESP32-S3. Screen updates appear when the CoreInk's e-ink would finish them: 0.26 s after the press, or after a 2 s flashing full refresh. Hold a button with the mouse or a key.

Space or Ttop button: timing click
Sside button: timing click; hold 3 s to switch off
↑ / ↓rocker up: made / rocker down: missed; competition mode: next / previous player
Enterrocker press: hold 0.5 s to undo; keep holding to undo more; press twice quickly: coach mode, switch page; competition mode, change your position
switched off: Sswitch on in competition mode
hold ↓ + Sswitch on in league mode
hold ↑ + Sswitch on in coach mode
