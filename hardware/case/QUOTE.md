# Request for quote: stoprock case (2 TPU parts)

A protective case for a small handheld timer (an M5Stack CoreInk, 40 x 56 x 16 mm). Two parts that
snap together around the device. **Prototype**: the first order is for fit testing, and the design
will likely change once printed.

## Files

| File | Part | Size (mm, as oriented in the file) | Volume | Approx. weight (TPU) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `stoprock-case-tub.stl` | Tub: back and walls, button pads, lanyard loop | 53.8 x 68.2 x 21.0 | 14.2 cm³ | 17 g |
| `stoprock-case-bezel.stl` | Bezel: front frame round the screen | 43.3 x 59.3 x 2.5 | 4.0 cm³ | 5 g |

Units are millimetres. Both files are already oriented for printing: the tub standing on its back,
the bezel face-down. `preview.png` shows both parts and how they fit.

## Material and finish

- **TPU, Shore 95A** (FDM), black or charcoal. If you also print TPU by MJF or SLS (about 88-90A),
  please quote that too, and say which you'd recommend for the features below.
- Solid parts: **100% infill** (or enough perimeters that the thin features are solid).
- Layer height 0.2 mm or finer. Standard as-printed finish; no painting.
- Tolerance ±0.2 mm or better where possible.

## Features to be aware of

- **Button pads** (top, and right side): each pad sits on a **0.8 mm web**, separated from the wall
  by a **1.0 mm gap**, so it can be pressed. The webs and gaps need to come out clean (not fused).
- **Snap fit**: the bezel has a 0.35 mm bead round its edge that clicks into a 0.4 mm groove inside
  the tub's rim.
- **Lanyard loop**: 3.5 mm thick ring with a 5 mm hole, on the tub's top corner. It will be pulled
  on, so it should be fully solid.
- Openings: a cut-out on the right side (20 x 9 mm), USB-C opening at the bottom (12.5 x 7 mm), and a
  1.8 mm pinhole in the back.
- Overhangs are small (pads and rims, up to 1.5 mm); we don't expect supports, but use them if you
  need to.
